FAQ

What can I do with fsocial?

Your profile is like a blog, an image feed or both, as you wish. You can post images, videos and text. You can create private or public profiles. You can find others and subscribe to them. Your feed will show their and your updates and nothing else.

Of course you can use hashtags (#) and you can mention others (@).

You also can post self-deleting updates. Known from various other services as "stories", we call them flowers. But you decide how long those flowers will bloom and when they wither. A few seconds, 24 hours or a few weeks? Up to you.

But we get rid of everything that doesn't serve any purpose than making you addicted and turning you into a product for corporations to abuse and advertise to. That means: We do not have useless likes or follower counters. They have nothing to do with being social and never served a cause other than pride. We put our focus on the content and we advise you to think before you post. If you post something, consider if it will help someone and make the world a better and more beautiful place or if it only serves your own selfish need for attention.

If you want to interact, you can react to posts, communicate and discuss via commentaries and replies, similar to a forum. The owner of the profile plays the role of the moderator. Thereby, this free social network can become a place of open discourse, while you remain in control of the commentaries on your profile. You can decide if you want to show them automatically or block them by default and unlock them manually. You can delete your profile and the data you published at any time - they were never on our servers.

Can I send private messages?

No. In this regard we follow the unix philosophy, according to which you should do one thing and do that right. The one thing we do is letting you create a social profile and fill it with content in the easiest way. Private messaging should not be part of this. If you want to message people, you should use Signal. In order to get in private contact with people, you can provide (optional) different contact possibilities on your profile, like an email-address, phone number (e.g. if you create a profile for a restaurant) etc.

Where is my profile and my content hosted?

Our idea is to provide you different options, but, in case of ressource hungry files like images, videos or audio, the content is not saved at the same place as your profile as such, but is split to different providers which in themselves are decentralised by design, according to the idea, that you rather should let them do their thing right and utilise their service than to re-invent the wheel.

In regard to your profile, we offer the following options:
GitHub Page: You can choose a convenient hosting (but with less control and privacy) to which we utilise services like GitHub Pages, where your profile is directly hosted on GitHub either with open or private access. In this case you depend on external servers just as with every other established social platform, but at least you can decide to delete your GitHub repo and thereby your profile via fsocial and you can check yourself by logging into your GitHub account if it is gone. But in this case, you need to trust GitHub (which is hold by Microsoft) and we do not recommend it. It's free of charge.
Your own webspace: If you want to have your profile hosted on your own webspace, you can choose 'Uberspace', a german, privacy focused webhoster that does not collect a single bit of data they don't need. But like with every serious webhosting, it is not free of charge. It will cost you around 5€ per month (we get nothing of it).
Your own server: If you are more privacy focused and want to have more control, you can set up your profile on your own server. This is for experienced users only who know what they do.

Your local device: But by far the easiest and at the same time one of the safest methods, which we recommend to begin with, is to stream your own profile from your local device. This means, your profile is streamed to others from your smartphone. Thereby it is really decentralised. Of course you can make backups of it and transfer it to another device as you wish. And if your profile contains nothing but text (like a blog), you won't need any external hosting.

In regard to your media content (images, video, audio etc.), they are hosted by different providers and are streamed from there. For example, videos can be automatically posted at lbry/odysee (in private) and when you delete them from your profile in the app, they will be deleted on lbry too. A decentralised alternative is peertube. This may sound complicated, but it isn't. The idea is, that rather than having douzens of platforms attempting similar things, we have one service that does one thing right. So there is one good platform for videos, one good platform for music, one good platform for images and fsocial is the interconnection between them.
And this system is not perfect, but we always try to endorse the platforms only that come closest to our philosophy of decentralisation, privacy etc.

Can't my content still be abused as on every other social media platform?

Absolutely. When you post something in public, be it a text, an image or a video, you should always be aware, that it probably will not be possible to delete it ever again, because anyone can always decide to safe it locally and upload and distribute it somewhere else. Everything you post in public, no matter on which platform, is never safe. It always can be copied in one way or another and this is nothing that we could change.

To prevent this is not a technical task, but a matter of education. Especially if you have children and let them use social media, you need to be aware of the risks. You need to be educated about the possible consequences of providing your child with a device that connects it to every other person in the world, including a high-resolution camera to film itself in every situation. While we cannot prevent this, we surely will not reject responsibility, but the only thing we can do about that, is attempting to educate you about it, see our education section. At the end, you are responsible for your child. This is a threat you need take into account and it is your responsibility to monitor your childrens media usage, if you let them use it at all. And of course, the same thing applies to yourself.

Who should use fsocial?

A social profile on fsocial is best suited for the following cases: You are a thinker/writer/scientist and want to post articles, ideas, essays, research and you want to communicate with people about them or initiate a discourse or you want to educate people with your content. You are an artist and you want to share images, photographies, short films, updates of your game etc. that you create(d). You are a musician and want to post music or provide updates to upcoming concerts etc. You have a business and want to create a profile to promote your product or service, provide contact information etc. You are an activist of any sort and want to use the platform to promote your ideas and spread the word of your activism.

Of course you can use it for every other purpose you wish. You can indeed post what you eat and make ugly selfies in your bikini or underpants, if you like to get attention, if you like to be stalked, if you like to make others jealous of your wealth or your body or your hypocritical lifestyle. Feel free to do so. But we won't reward you for filling the platform with gargabe like that through likes or follower statistics which you could keep track of and show off with. We don't have this asocial shit, nor will we promote your profile on our index in such a case, because we only list things which are social, non-commercial, useful, educational and uplifting.
