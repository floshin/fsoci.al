Our Philosophy

We are highly critical of established social media platforms. They are constantly abusing you. They create profiles from your data and sell it, they manipulate you, they make you addicted and the usage of their platforms go along with ugly consequences like cyber-bullying, inferiority complexes, distorted self-perception etc. How social media evolved to be, sucks incredibly. But it doesn't need to be like that.

In response to the critique, we formed a non-profit social business, to attempt to build an alternative without abuse, manipulation, misanthropic algorhithms and free from profit interests.

You offer the content, we offer the framework. We provide an index with content that is educative, help- and beautiful and things that you promote, not what people with money pay for. And on your feed you only see the content you subscribed for. Nothing else. We give the control back to the host, you.

Fuck established social media, that abuses you for profit. Social media needs to be non-commercial on principle, and that is what fsocial is about.
