# Indexed pages

The central page, the "index" of fsocial, will contain links to personal websites. 
At the top there may be tags/selectors such as:

Craft | Security | Spirituality | Philosophy | Art | Poetry | Music | Sports | Apps

There shouldn't be too many; they are limited to the most crucial areas of 
human life and only to those areas which can be considered to be valuable,
peaceful etc. The websites linked have to comply to some basic standards;
* avoiding scripts and cookies 
* not filled with ads
* it must be non-commercial OR it must be a single-person (craftsmen, so no links to companies)
* etc. 

Then we select websites and have one subpage (e.g. via jekyll) for each such category.
In each category, there will be pictures, each of the same size for simplicites sake,
nicely scaled by css to every device (desktop, mobile etc.).

Example, Spirituality:
* Vinoba Bhave
* Hillside Hermitage

* a good free ressource or book about jainism etc.
* links to the most essential freely available books
* etc.
