A free, decentralised & non-profit
p2p social network that sucks less.

  • Your profile - your static website. Self-hosted or safed and backed up on your local device(s) - a blog, image-, video- and audio-feed.
  • A social platform without abuse: No ads, no censorship, no algorhitm, no manipulation. You only see what you subscribed for, nothing else.
  • Without distraction and addictive mechanisms: There are no likes nor follower counts. Social media reduced to its essence.
