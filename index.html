How to start?

Everything starts with a simple decision. If we recognise that we find something wrong, if we do not want to support it, then we can just stop doing so. If established social media is abusing us for profit, then just stop using their products, stop feeding them.

Taking responsibility begins by not closing our eyes when reality is hard to endure. But only if we become aware of what is going on can we start to take responsibility. While our entire digital communication may now depend on mega corporations, this is no necessity. More digital independence and freedom is possible. We only have to be ready to endure a little bit of inconvenience to overcome the convenient slavery into which we have navigated ourselves.

They hold us captive to mine our communication for data and to sell us useless stuff. In the end they give our data to autocratic regimes that turn them against us as they wish. If you aren't fond of this idea then do something about it by starting with yourself.
